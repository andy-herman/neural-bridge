# South Korean telecom security incidents 2023-2026 and their consequences

Method note: all outbound page fetches from this sandbox were refused by the proxy (403 CONNECT on korea.net, koreatimes, hankyung, sbs, boannews, etc.), so every finding below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited articles rather than full-text reads. Figures were cross-checked across at least two extracts where possible; single-source figures are flagged. "Official" = Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) joint public-private investigation team, Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC) decisions, or Korea.kr ministry briefings. "Press" = reporting not attributed to an official release. Korean titles are given with an English gloss in brackets.

## Key Question 1: For each incident, dates, dwell time, scale, root causes, fines, compensation, leadership consequences

### Takeaway
All three carriers were breached within 2023-2025, each on roughly the same pattern: years-long dwell time on management servers, plaintext credentials or default passwords, late or absent reporting, then a record-setting PIPC fine (LGU+ 6.8bn won 2023; SKT 134.8bn won 2025; KT 54.0bn won 2026). SKT and KT both lost their CEOs (reassigned/not reappointed rather than fired), ran termination-fee waivers that bled 200k-600k subscribers, and pledged 700bn-1tn won security programs over five years. No new carrier breach was found in 2026; the 2026 wave hit banks and MVNOs.

### Cited Findings

#### 1A. SK Telecom USIM/HSS breach (disclosed April 2025)

Timeline and dwell time (official MSIT joint-investigation findings, 4 July 2025):
- SKT detected abnormally large outbound traffic at 23:20 on 18 April 2025 and notified KISA at 16:46 on 20 April 2025, past the 24-hour statutory window; MSIT formed the public-private joint investigation team on 23 April 2025 — [MSIT release via Korea.net](https://www.korea.net/Government/Briefing-Room/Press-Releases/view?articleId=8120&type=O&insttCode=)
- The joint team dated the first malware implant to 6 August 2021: the attacker installed CrossC2 (remote-control backdoor) on a system-management-network server reachable from outside, about 10 months earlier than the May interim estimate — [이투데이 "SKT, 서버 33종 악성코드 감염…해킹 첫 공격 2021년 8월" (SKT servers infected with 33 malware types; first attack Aug 2021)](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2484884); [파이낸셜뉴스 "SKT 해킹 4년전 시작…악성코드 33종 확인" (SKT hack began 4 years ago; 33 malware types confirmed)](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202507041401021258)
- The first compromised server held IDs and passwords for other servers in plaintext; the attacker used them to reach a second server and obtain the account for the HSS (subscriber authentication) management server, installing BPFDoor there on 24 December 2021. The team noted the ISMS certification guide already told operators not to store server credentials in plaintext — [데이터넷 "SKT 해킹, 계정관리 소홀한 서버에서 시작" (SKT hack started from a server with lax account management)](https://www.datanet.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=203442); [이비엔 "4년에 걸친 침투…총 2696만 건 유출" (A four-year intrusion; 26.96m records leaked)](https://www.ebn.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=1669316)
- SKT's own internal review in February 2022 found abnormal login attempts on the HSS management server but checked only one of six log records, missed the attacker's sessions, found no malware and did not report to the authorities; the team said a report then would have enabled a government investigation — [매일일보 "SKT 해킹 2021년부터 시작…2022년 발견하고도 숨겨" (SKT hack started 2021; found in 2022 but hidden)](https://www.m-i.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=1256811); [뉴스프리존 "SKT, 2021년부터 해커 공격 받아...과실 책임 물어 위약금 면제" (SKT attacked since 2021; fault found, fee waiver)](https://www.newsfreezone.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=634664)
- Dwell time therefore runs from 6 Aug 2021 (first implant) / 24 Dec 2021 (HSS access) to 18 Apr 2025 (exfiltration detected): roughly 3 years 8 months. The team also said some periods had no logs, so whether call records were exfiltrated earlier cannot be confirmed — [이투데이](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2484884)

Scale (official):
- All 42,605 SKT servers were scanned; 28 were infected with 33 malware strains (27 BPFDoor variants, 3 TinyShell, 1 WebShell, 2 open-source C2 frameworks CrossC2 and Sliver). 25 categories of USIM data totalling 9.82 GB, about 26.96 million IMSI records, were exfiltrated — [MSIT release via Korea.net](https://www.korea.net/Government/Briefing-Room/Press-Releases/view?articleId=8120&type=O&insttCode=); [e4ds "SKT, SIM card information leaked 26,957,749 cases"](https://www.e4ds.com/sub_view.asp?idx=20866&lang=en)
- The May 2025 interim count was 23 infected servers and 25 malware types (24 BPFDoor + 1 webshell); the July final raised it to 28 and 33 — [Korea.net](https://www.korea.net/Government/Briefing-Room/Press-Releases/view?articleId=8120&type=O&insttCode=)
- PIPC counted affected data subjects as 23,244,649 (all LTE/5G users incl. MVNO, de-duplicated), with 25 items incl. phone number, IMSI and USIM authentication key — [한국경제 "개인정보위, SK텔레콤에 역대 최대 과징금 1348억 부과" (PIPC levies record 134.8bn won fine on SKT)](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202508287209g). Note the two numbers measure different things: 26.96m IMSI records vs 23.24m unique people.
- Press reported that two servers were submitted in a state that made forensics impossible despite a preservation order, and that SKT faced a fine of up to 30m won for the late report — [이로운넷 "'고객 책임 없다' 정부, SKT 유심 해킹에 '위약금 면제' 최종 판단" (Government: customers not at fault; final termination-fee waiver ruling)](https://www.eroun.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=59036); [Dark Reading "South Korea Imposes Penalties on SK Telecom for Breach"](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/south-korea-imposes-penalties-sk-telecom-breach). Whether the 30m won fine was actually collected was not confirmed in any extract (see Gaps).

Root causes named (official):
- MSIT: plaintext server credentials, poor account management, insufficient supply-chain security, short security-log retention; SKT "failed the duty of care expected of an ordinary operator," which the ministry classed as clear fault, triggering the termination-fee waiver clause — [뉴스프리존](https://www.newsfreezone.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=634664); [이로운넷](https://www.eroun.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=59036)
- PIPC (28 Aug 2025): internal, internet and management networks shared one network (no segmentation); USIM authentication keys stored unencrypted in plaintext; breach notification deficiencies; first intrusion dated August 2021; 9.82 GB taken from the HSS database on 18 April 2025 — [한국경제](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202508287209g); [서울경제 "'유심 해킹' SKT에 역대 최대 과징금…개인정보위, 1348억 원 부과"](https://www.sedaily.com/NewsView/2GWSV4FO9M)

Fines:
- PIPC plenary 28 Aug 2025: administrative surcharge 134,791,000,000 won (reported as 1,348억) plus 9.6m won administrative fine, for safeguarding-duty and notification violations; largest single-company penalty since PIPC's 2020 launch; base = SKT's total mobile-service revenue; violation graded "very serious." Corrective orders: review of all mobile personal-data processing, CPO-centred governance; recommendation to extend ISMS-P certification scope to the mobile network systems — [한국경제](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202508287209g); [소비자가만드는신문 "유심 해킹 SK텔레콤, 과징금 1348억...피해 보상 노력으로 최악은 피했다" (SKT fined 134.8bn; compensation efforts averted the worst)](https://www.consumernews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=739440)
- Industry had expected up to the mid-300bn-won range; the lower figure was attributed to SKT's compensation measures (fee waiver etc.). Some experts argued the fine was excessive given no confirmed secondary harm and no unjust profit; it exceeded prior records against Google (69.2bn) and Meta (30.8bn) — [소비자가만드는신문](https://www.consumernews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=739440)
- SKT filed suit at Seoul Administrative Court on 19 January 2026 to annul the surcharge, one day before the filing deadline — [네이트/연합 "'개인정보 유출' SKT, 개인정보위 상대로 1348억 과징금 취소 소송 제기"](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260119n34334); [보안뉴스 "SKT, 개인정보 유출 과징금 1347억원 취소 소송 제기"](https://m.boannews.com/html/detail.html?idx=141569)

SIM replacement and sales halt (press, with SKT daily briefings):
- 280,000 SIMs replaced on the first day of the free-swap program — [보안뉴스 "SKT 유심 교체 하루 만에 28만명"](https://m.boannews.com/html/detail.html?idx=137036)
- By 7 May 2025: 100% enrolment in USIM Protection Service and 1.07m SIMs replaced — [보안뉴스 "유심보호서비스 100% 가입 완료..유심 교체는 107만건"](https://m.boannews.com/html/detail.html?idx=137145)
- By 29 May 2025: 5.17m replaced, 3.89m still on the waiting list, completion targeted for June — [신아일보 "'SKT 해킹 사태' 유심 교체 517만명"](https://www.shinailbo.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=2060191)
- SKT suspended new-subscriber sales at ~2,600 T world stores from 2 May 2025 to prioritise swaps; sales resumed after ~50 days (reports vary 46-51 days) — [뉴시스 "해킹사고에 SKT는 '50일간 영업 중단'·KT는 '無 중단'…왜 다를까" (Why SKT halted sales 50 days and KT did not)](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20251229_0003458268); [이데일리 "SKT, 50일 만에 영업 재개…이탈 가입자 60만명 '상흔'"](https://www.edaily.co.kr/News/Read?mediaCodeNo=257&newsId=03640806642204672)
- MVNO/number-porting incentives for SIM-only switchers reportedly rose from 100-150k won to 300k won after the breach — [블로터 "[위약금 면제의 역설]①"](https://www.bloter.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=651045)

Compensation (company measures, press):
- 4 July 2025 "책임과 약속" (Responsibility and Promise) program: termination-fee waiver 5-14 July for contracts signed before 18 April (handset instalments excluded); refunds for fees already paid; "customer appreciation package" worth ~500bn won for ~24m SKT and SKT-network MVNO users: 50% off the August 2025 bill (auto-applied) and 50 GB extra data per month August-December, plus expanded T-membership partner discounts — [한국경제 "SKT 7월14일까지 위약금 면제…남은 고객은 반값 할인"](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202507048999i); [파이낸셜뉴스 "SKT, 위약금 면제 요구 수용…정보보호 투자에 5년간 7000억원"](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202507041558132337)
- Korea Consumer Agency dispute committee (meeting 18 Dec 2025, announced 21 Dec): SKT should pay 100,000 won per victim, structured as 50,000 won bill credit plus 50,000 T-plus points; 58 applicants, but ~2.3tn won if extended to all ~23m victims — [네이트/연합 "소비자위 'SKT 해킹 피해자에 10만원씩 보상'"](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20251221n07110)
- SKT rejected that mediation on 30 January 2026, citing >1tn won already spent on compensation and security plus the 134.8bn fine; it had earlier rejected the PIPC dispute committee's 300,000-won-per-person proposal; KCA is weighing litigation support for applicants — [뉴스1 "SKT, 해킹피해 1인당 10만원 보상안 불수용"](https://www.news1.kr/it-science/cc-newmedia/6057212); [네이트 "SKT, 해킹 피해 '1인 10만원' 조정안 불수용…소비자원 '집단분쟁조정 소송 지원 검토'"](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260130n29541)

Subscriber churn and financial impact (press):
- 217,542 SKT subscribers left during the 10-day waiver window — [한국경제](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202507048999i)
- By mid-June 2025, 568,572 had left since the breach, net loss 491,606 — [이데일리](https://www.edaily.co.kr/News/Read?mediaCodeNo=257&newsId=03640806642204672); [헤럴드경제 "50만명 이탈 SKT, 유심 교체 19일 완료"](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10512045)
- Dark Reading reported SKT expected the measures to cost about 700bn won (US$511m) in revenue — [Dark Reading](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/south-korea-imposes-penalties-sk-telecom-breach); a Korean rating-agency estimate put combined SIM, fine and subsidy outlays above 400bn won — [CEO스코어데일리 "SKT, 유심해킹 여파 2분기 실적 '먹구름'"](https://www.ceoscoredaily.com/page/view/2025050716331413848)
- Q3 2025 operating profit fell more than 90% YoY, with the PIPC surcharge booked as expense — [중앙일보/코리아데일리 "'해킹 사고'에 영업익 90% 날아간 SKT…사령탑 교체"](https://www.koreadaily.com/article/20251030001641161)
- FY2025 consolidated revenue 17.0992tn won (-4.7%) and operating profit 1.0732tn won (-41.1%); SKT said the hack's impact was mostly absorbed in 2025 — [네이트/뉴스1 "'해킹 여파' SKT, 2025년 수익성 악화"](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260205n34427)

Leadership:
- CEO Ryu Young-sang told the National Assembly hearing he agreed it was "the worst hack in telecom history" and admitted the early response was inadequate — [뉴스1 "과방위, 유영상 SKT 대표 질타"](https://www.news1.kr/politics/assembly/5769735); [CEO스코어데일리 "유영상 SKT 대표 '통신 역사상 최악의 해킹'"](https://www.ceoscoredaily.com/page/view/2025043017314448659)

#### 1B. KT 2025: rogue femtocells, micro-payment fraud, concealed server infections

Timeline (official unless noted):
- Victims clustered geographically (26 users in one Gwangmyeong neighbourhood hit simultaneously, all KT) in late August/early September 2025 — [보안뉴스 "광명 한 동네 사는 26명, 스마트폰 소액결제 동시다발 피해"](https://m.boannews.com/html/detail.html?idx=139089) (press)
- KT recognised the anomaly on 5 September 2025 but reported the incident only on 8 September, after an outside security firm confirmed intrusion traces — [데일리시큐 "KT 해킹, 불법 펨토셀·서버 악성코드·지연신고…정부 중간조사로 드러난 3중 실패" (Triple failure revealed by interim probe)](https://www.dailysecu.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=202267)
- Police arrested a Chinese-Korean suspect in his 40s and seized illegal base-station equipment — [보안뉴스 "[KT 무단 소액결제] 40대 중국 교포 용의자 체포...불법 기지국 장비 확보"](https://m.boannews.com/html/detail.html?idx=139318) (press)
- 17 October 2025: KT raised the count of rogue femtocell IDs from 4 to 20 and victims from 362 to 368, and said leak indicators dated back to October 2024 — [보안뉴스 "KT, 불법 펨토셀 ID 총 20개 확인...작년 10월부터 개인정보 유출 정황"](https://m.boannews.com/html/detail.html?idx=139808)
- Interim result 6 November 2025: KT had found 43 servers infected with BPFDoor and webshells between March and July 2024, cleaned them itself and never reported; those servers held names, phone numbers, emails and IMEIs. Traces had been wiped, so the post-SKT nationwide BPFDoor scan missed them; antivirus-run artefacts exposed it. No USIM-key leak confirmed at that point — [Korea.kr 부처 브리핑 "KT 침해사고 중간조사 결과" (KT incident interim findings)](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/policyBriefingView.do?newsId=156726252); [대구신문 "KT, 작년 서버 해킹 알고도 은폐 정황"](https://www.idaegu.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=528804)
- Final result 29 December 2025: an illegal femtocell connected to the core network leaked IMSI, IMEI and phone numbers of 22,227 subscribers; 368 of them suffered 777 unauthorised micro-payments worth about 243m won (gift-card purchases using intercepted auth codes). All femtocells shared one manufacturer certificate with 10-year validity, so a single prior connection allowed persistent core-network access. KT said it had discarded suspect servers on 1 August but the actual timing differed, and it withheld existing backup logs from investigators. Total 94 servers infected with 103 malware types (BPFDoor, rootkits); 41 of them self-remediated in 2024 without report. The team cited "insufficient basic information-protection activities": weak checks, missing security equipment, short log retention. Eavesdropping on calls was judged technically possible. The ministry ruled KT at fault, said the fee waiver should apply to all KT subscribers, flagged a fine of up to 30m won for late reporting, and referred obstruction to police — [Korea.kr 부처 브리핑 "KT/LGU+ 침해사고 조사 결과 관련 브리핑"](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/policyBriefingView.do?newsId=156737260); [산업일보 "과기정통부 'KT 침해사고, 회사 과실'…KT 전체 이용자 위약금 면제"](https://kidd.co.kr/news/244467); [네이트/연합 "KT 해킹 '도청'도 가능했다…'전 가입자 위약금 면제해야'"](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20251229n27044); [NK경제 "과기정통부 'KT 서버에서 악성코드 103종에 감염 확인'"](https://www.nkeconomy.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=15737)
- Korea Times reported (Feb 2026) that KT paid 26.25m won in administrative fines for three counts of cyber-incident reporting violations — [Korea Times "KT, LG Uplus face lingering fallout over hacking incidents"](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20260228/kt-lg-uplus-face-lingering-fallout-over-hacking-incidents) (single-source; a Korean-language search could not re-find this figure)

Government-mandated remediation (official, 29 Dec 2025):
- New femtocell connections blocked across all three carriers; KT's femtocell certificate validity cut from 10 years to 1 month; KT ordered to keep end-to-end IPSec from handset to core enabled and monitor for disablement and abnormal traffic; KT to submit an implementation plan by January 2026, implement by April 2026, with MSIT verification by June 2026 and corrective orders under Network Act art. 48-4 if needed — [쿠키뉴스 "정부, KT 가입자 전원 위약금 면제 요구…LG유플러스 서버 폐기 공무집행방해 경찰 수사"](https://www.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202512290168); [Korea.kr 브리핑](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/policyBriefingView.do?newsId=156737260)

KT compensation (company, press):
- Earlier (Oct 2025) KT waived termination fees only for the 368 micro-payment victims — [보안뉴스 "KT, '무단 소액결제 해킹 피해자에 위약금 면제'"](https://m.boannews.com/html/detail.html?idx=139871)
- 30 December 2025: CEO Kim Young-shub apologised "in repentance"; all-subscriber termination-fee waiver 31 Dec 2025 - 13 Jan 2026; 100 GB extra data per month for 6 months; free "safety insurance" against hacking/phishing for 2 years (auto-enrolled for seniors); package valued ~450bn won; 1tn won + alpha security investment over 5 years; new company-wide Information Security Innovation TF — [ZDNet Korea "KT, 2주간 위약금 면제...4500억 규모 보상안 가동"](https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20251230175221); [네이트/연합 "KT '장기 혜택 중심 4500억 보상…향후 5년간 1조 원+α 보안 투자'"](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20251230n31766)
- Users criticised the absence of a direct bill discount and the 2-week window — [줌뉴스 "KT 해킹 보상안 나왔다...반응은"](https://m.news.zum.com/articles/103140022/)
- Churn: ~50,000 left in the first 4 days, 61-71% to SKT (partly returning family-bundle customers who had left SKT in 2025) — [머니투데이 "KT, 위약금 면제 나흘간 5만명 이탈…'61%가 SKT로'"](https://www.mt.co.kr/tech/2026/01/04/2026010408182734592); ~210,000 over the full waiver, breaking the three-way balance in SKT's favour — [네이트/연합 "KT 위약금 면제 21만명 이탈…3사 균형 깨지고 SKT '쏠림'"](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260111n09322); KTOA count >238,000 after the waiver — [Korea Times, 28 Feb 2026](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20260228/kt-lg-uplus-face-lingering-fallout-over-hacking-incidents)

## Key Question 2: Systemic weaknesses the government investigations found common to the carriers

### Takeaway
Official findings across LGU+ (2023), SKT (2025) and KT (2025) repeat five items: end-of-life or unpatched systems; plaintext credentials or unchanged default passwords on management servers; no network segmentation and no real-time monitoring of bulk outbound data; short log retention that defeated forensics; and reporting failures (late reports, self-remediation without disclosure, evidence destruction). Parliament's research service and academics added a sixth: outsourced, under-funded security teams.

### Cited Findings
- LGU+ 2023 (PIPC/MSIT): all commercial software on the leaked system (OS, DBMS, web, WAS) was discontinued or unsupported at the time of the leak; admin default password unchanged; no automated real-time outbound monitoring; routers exposed without IPS; cause summarised as lack of investment and staff — [경향신문](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202307122209025); [머니투데이 2023-04-27](https://www.mt.co.kr/tech/2023/04/27/2023042710035852256)
- SKT 2025 (MSIT/PIPC): plaintext inter-server credentials and USIM keys; no separation of internal/internet/management networks; account-management negligence; weak supply-chain security; short log retention; 2022 internal detection not reported — [데이터넷](https://www.datanet.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=203442); [한국경제](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202508287209g); [매일일보](https://www.m-i.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=1256811)
- KT 2025 (MSIT/PIPC): shared 10-year certificate for all femtocells; no source-IP restriction; management-server bypass path; no rogue Cell-ID detection; "insufficient basic security activities" (checks, equipment, short log retention); 41-43 infected servers self-cleaned without report in 2024; logs deleted and false statements during probe — [Korea.kr 브리핑](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/policyBriefingView.do?newsId=156737260); [머니투데이 2026-07-30](https://www.mt.co.kr/tech/2026/07/30/2026073007503981141)
- Reporting failures as a pattern: SKT reported ~41 hours after detection (24h limit); KT reported 3 days after; LGU+ reported 3 months after KISA's notice and only after a parliamentary hearing — [Korea.net](https://www.korea.net/Government/Briefing-Room/Press-Releases/view?articleId=8120&type=O&insttCode=); [데일리시큐](https://www.dailysecu.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=202267); [서울신문 2025-10-24](https://www.seoul.co.kr/news/economy/industry/2025/10/24/20251024016002)
- Budget: at the 2025 hearing, SKT (23.07m subscribers) had spent 60bn won on infosec in 2024 vs KT 121.8bn (13.36m subs) and LGU+ 63.2bn (10.94m subs); per subscriber 2,400 / 6,700 / 4,000 won; SKT's infosec spend was 3.29% of operating profit vs KT 15% and LGU+ 7.34%; SKT's spend had fallen 4.3% from 62.7bn in 2022 — [보안뉴스 "SKT 600억, KT 1218억..이통사 보안 투자 도마 올라"](https://m.boannews.com/html/detail.html?idx=137070&tab_type=1); [경향신문 "SKT 광고비는 1367억원, 보안투자는 867억원"](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202505041204001); [문화일보 "아이돌 모델 쓰며 광고선전비 1367억 쓴 SKT, 정보보호 투자는 600억"](https://www.munhwa.com/article/11503305)
- Headcount/outsourcing: in 2025 disclosures SKT (incl. SK Broadband) had 77.28% of dedicated infosec staff outsourced, highest of the three; LGU+ 140.9 internal vs 210.4 outsourced; KT 164.8 internal vs 152.3 outsourced — [이투데이 "통신3사, 정보보호 투자 22% 늘려…보안인력은 여전히 외주 의존"](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2600214); [이코리아 "해킹 사고 겪은 통신사, 보안 투자 내역 살펴보니"](https://www.ekoreanews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=86816)
- National Assembly Research Service (NARS) report: carrier hacks are structural, not episodic (KT 2012, 2014; LGU+ 2023; SKT 2025); SKT's HSS servers were outside the designated "critical information and communications infrastructure" despite national-level impact; ISMS-P certification did not prevent the breach — [디지털투데이 "반복되는 이통사 해킹 대응 보안 강화 어떻게?...국회입법조사처 보고서"](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=567251); [포인트데일리 "입법조사처 '통신사 정보보호 투자 의무화하고 과징금 부과 근거 마련해야'"](https://www.pointdaily.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=248626); [NARS report via KDI](https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/domesticView.do?ac=0000195055)

- MSIT's emergency on-site checks of KT and LGU+ after SKT (from late May 2025) found "no anomalies so far" in June 2025, yet KT's 2024 infections were later confirmed, because KT had wiped traces — [이투데이 "과기정통부, KTㆍLG유플러스 보안 점검…현재까지 특이사항 없어"](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2476192); [Korea.kr 중간조사](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/policyBriefingView.do?newsId=156726252)

### Inferences
- The sector-wide BPFDoor sweep in mid-2025 produced a false clean bill for KT, which implies the government's inspection model depends on carrier candour; the 2026 Network Act's ex officio investigation power is a direct response.
- Spending per subscriber inverted the breach severity: the carrier spending least per user (SKT) had the largest leak, which is why lawmakers pushed mandatory infosec-budget ratios.

### Gaps
- No official document was found that explicitly aggregates the three carriers' weaknesses into one government "common findings" list; the pattern above is assembled from the separate MSIT/PIPC findings.
- "Unpatched servers" is explicit for LGU+ (EOL software) but for SKT the official extracts stress credentials, segmentation and logging rather than specific missing patches.

## Key Question 3: How the PIPC's revised penalty regime (up to 3% of related revenue) applied, and whether fines were seen as deterrent or trivial

### Takeaway
The 2023 PIPA amendment moved the cap from 3% of related revenue to 3% of total revenue; LGU+ was fined under the old base (6.8bn won), SKT under the new one (134.8bn won on total mobile revenue), KT at 54bn won. Korean commentary split: consumer advocates and PIPC called the fines historic, carriers and some academics called them excessive or double-penalising, and a 2026 PIPA amendment raised the ceiling to 10% for repeat/mass breaches, signalling that regulators judged 3% insufficient.

## Key Question 5: What critics in Korea said about why telecoms, as critical infrastructure, were this exposed

### Takeaway
Korean critics converged on four explanations: carriers treated security as a cost centre (SKT spent more than twice as much on advertising as on security), relied on outsourced staff with no one owning the end-to-end picture, hid behind certifications (ISMS-P) that measured paperwork rather than defence, and operated under a legal regime where the government could not investigate without a self-report and the penalties for silence were smaller than the penalties for candour.

### Cited Findings
- Hankyung (29 Dec 2025) headline summarised the sentiment: "Korea has become a hackers' playground; all three carriers breached" — [한국경제 "해커 놀이터 된 한국…통신3사 모두 뚫렸다"](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025122909161)
- Advertising vs security: SKT spent 136.7bn won on advertising (idol models) against 60bn won on infosec in 2024 — [문화일보](https://www.munhwa.com/article/11503305); [경향신문](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202505041204001)
